Data notes and limitations

  • All figures are gross drug cost (Medicare, plan and beneficiary payments). Manufacturer rebates are not public, so net spend and net savings are lower.
  • Part D covers drugs patients take themselves. Clinic-administered drugs (Part B) are out of scope.
  • The current CMS release appears to drop drugs no longer dispensed, so earlier years run about 1% below figures published from older releases; 2020 to 2024 growth is slightly overstated.
  • Price per unit is gross spend divided by CMS dosage units, within each drug. Shifts between strengths of the same drug show up as price.
  • Insulin spend fell by half in 2024, after manufacturers cut list prices. The forecast treats it as a new level.
